DATACIRCLES - EMAIL TEMPLATES
================================================================
All mails are sent via SendGrid (backend/utils/sendGridMail.js)
from "DataCircles <noreply@datacircles.in>" unless noted.
All use one common layout (backend/utils/emailLayout.js):
  logo header > greeting > body > [button] > "Regards" > footer
  (DataCircles Technology, Thane address, phone, website, GSTIN)

Legend:  <x> = filled in automatically    [Button] = clickable button

IMPORTANT: There is NO email for plan purchase, autopay renewal,
payment failure, add-on purchase or signup welcome. These do not
exist in the code yet.

1. SUBSCRIPTION & BILLING
################################################################

1.1  TRIAL STARTED
  When     : New organisation's free trial begins (or super admin starts one)
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles trial is active
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Your 7-day free trial for <organisation> is now active, with full
    access to all <Plan> plan features.
    Your trial ends on <date>. You can choose a plan at any time from
    Settings > Subscription, and your data and setup carry over.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.2  TRIAL ENDING SOON
  When     : Automatic, 48 hours and again 24 hours before trial end (once each)
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles trial ends on <date>
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Your free trial for <organisation> ends in about <48/24> hours, on <date>.
    To keep adding and editing data after that, choose a plan. Your
    existing data stays available to view either way.
    [Choose a plan]
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.3  TRIAL EXPIRED
  When     : Automatic, when the trial end date passes
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles trial has ended
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Your free trial for <organisation> has ended. Your data is safe and
    you can still sign in to view everything you've added.
    To start adding and editing again, choose a plan that fits your team.
    [Choose a plan]
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.4  RENEWAL DUE (manual-billing plans only)
  When     : Automatic, on the renewal date
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles renewal of <Rs amount> is due
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Your <Plan> plan for <organisation> renews on <date>. The amount due
    is <Rs amount> (incl. GST).
    Pay any time before <date + 7 days> with UPI, card, net banking or
    wallet. After that the workspace becomes read-only until the payment
    is made.
    [Pay now]
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.5  RENEWAL OVERDUE REMINDER (manual-billing plans only)
  When     : Automatic, 3 days and 6 days after the due date if unpaid
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Reminder: DataCircles renewal of <Rs amount> is overdue
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Your renewal payment of <Rs amount> was due on <date>.
    Please pay before <date + 7 days> to avoid your workspace becoming
    read-only.
    [Pay now]
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.6  WORKSPACE READ-ONLY (manual-billing plans only)
  When     : Automatic, 7 days after the due date if still unpaid
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles workspace is now read-only
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    The renewal payment of <Rs amount> due on <date> hasn't been
    received, so your workspace is now read-only.
    Your data is safe. Pay the renewal to restore full access immediately.
    [Pay now]
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.7  TRIAL EXTENDED / REDUCED BY SUPPORT
  When     : Super admin changes the trial days
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles trial now ends on <new date>
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Our support team has <extended/reduced> the free trial for
    <organisation> by <N> day(s).
    Your trial now ends on <new date>.
    Questions? Reply to this email or contact support@datacircles.in.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.8  TRIAL ENDED BY SUPPORT
  When     : Super admin ends the trial immediately
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles trial has ended
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    Our support team has ended the free trial for <organisation>. Your
    data is safe and remains available to view.
    To continue adding and editing data, choose a plan.
    [Choose a plan]
    Questions? Contact support@datacircles.in.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.9  SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLED BY SUPPORT
  When     : Super admin cancels a trial or paid subscription
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admin
  Subject  : Your DataCircles subscription has been cancelled
  Body (trial):
    Hello <name>,
    Our support team has cancelled the trial for <organisation>. Your
    data stays available to view in read-only mode.
    If this was unexpected, contact support@datacircles.in and we'll be
    glad to help.
    Regards, Team DataCircles
  Body (paid plan):
    Hello <name>,
    Our support team has cancelled the DataCircles subscription for
    <organisation>.
    You'll keep full access until <period end date>. After that, your
    data stays available to view in read-only mode.
    If this was unexpected, contact support@datacircles.in and we'll be
    glad to help.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.10 [INTERNAL] NEW FREE TRIAL STARTED
  When     : Any organisation's free trial starts (self-serve, or started
             by super admin)
  From->To : DataCircles -> yash.mishra@datacircles.in
             (env SALES_ALERT_EMAIL, comma-separated for more people)
  Subject  : New free trial started: <organisation>
  Body     :
    A new customer has started a free trial on DataCircles.
      Organisation / Name / Contact / Plan trialled / Trial ends
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.11 [INTERNAL] NEW SUBSCRIPTION PURCHASED
  When     : An organisation's first subscription payment is confirmed
             (sent once per subscription)
  From->To : DataCircles -> yash.mishra@datacircles.in
             (env SALES_ALERT_EMAIL, comma-separated for more people)
  Subject  : New subscription purchased: <organisation> (<Plan>)
  Body     :
    A customer has bought a DataCircles subscription.
      Organisation / Admin / Contact / Plan / Billing cycle / Amount /
      Purchased on
    Regards, Team DataCircles

1.12 [INTERNAL] NEW ACCOUNT CREATED
  When     : Someone signs up and creates a new organisation (email signup,
             Google/phone registration). Not sent when a person joins an
             existing organisation.
  From->To : DataCircles -> yash.mishra@datacircles.in (env SALES_ALERT_EMAIL)
  Subject  : New account created: <organisation>
  Body     :
    A new account has been created on DataCircles.
      Organisation / Name / Contact / Phone / Created on
    Regards, Team DataCircles


################################################################
2. ACCOUNT & LOGIN
################################################################

2.1  VERIFICATION CODE (OTP)
  When     : Signup email check; Profile > verify email; Profile > change
             email; confirming an email/phone change request
  From->To : DataCircles -> the email being verified
  Subject  : Your DataCircles verification code
  Body     :
    [Hello <name>,]   (not shown during signup)
    Use this code to verify your email address and finish setting up your
    DataCircles account:      (wording varies slightly per case)
        [ 6-DIGIT CODE ]
    This code expires in 10 minutes. If you didn't request it, you can
    ignore this email.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

2.2  RESET PASSWORD
  When     : User clicks "Forgot password"
  From->To : DataCircles -> that user
  Subject  : Reset your DataCircles password
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    We received a request to reset the password for your DataCircles
    account. Enter the code below to choose a new one, or use the button.
        [ 6-DIGIT CODE ]
    [Reset password]
    The code expires in 10 minutes and the link in 1 hour. If you didn't
    make this request, no action is needed and your password stays the same.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

2.3  TEAM INVITATION
  When     : Admin invites a colleague (if an extra paid seat is needed,
             sent only after that payment succeeds)
  From->To : DataCircles -> invited person
  Subject  : <inviter> has invited you to join <organisation> on DataCircles
  Body     :
    <inviter> has invited you to join <organisation> on DataCircles, the
    CRM the team uses to manage contacts, deals and day-to-day sales work.
    Once you accept, you'll be able to work with <organisation>'s
    contacts, companies and deals, shared sales pipelines and team
    workflows, real-time collaboration, and reporting and analytics tools.
    [Accept invitation]
    (fallback link text) This invitation link is unique to you. If you
    weren't expecting it, you can ignore this email.
    New to DataCircles? Sign in with Google, Facebook, GitHub or LinkedIn,
    or create an account with your phone number.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

2.4  NEW TEAM MEMBER JOINED
  When     : Invited person accepts and joins
  From->To : DataCircles -> all admins of that organisation
  Subject  : <staff name> has joined <organisation>
  Body     :
    <staff name> (<email/phone>) has accepted their invitation and is now
    a member of <organisation> on DataCircles.
      Team member / Contact / Organisation
    Review or adjust what they can access from Settings > User Management.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

2.5  DELETE ACCOUNT / RESET DATA REQUEST
  When     : User submits the request in settings
  From->To : DataCircles -> organisation admins + support email
  Subject  : Account deletion request - <organisation>
             (or) Data reset request - <organisation>
  Body     :
    <requester> (<contact>) has requested to <delete the account / reset
    account data> for <organisation> on DataCircles.
      Requested by / Contact / Organisation / Request type
    Delete: This is irreversible - please verify the request with the
    organisation before acting on it. Processed within 5-7 business days.
    Reset: This wipes all companies, deals, contacts and other records -
    please verify first. Processed within 5-7 business days.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

3. REFERRAL & WALLET
################################################################

3.1  REFERRAL INVITATION
  When     : Customer enters a friend's email under Refer & Earn
  From->To : DataCircles (for the referring user) -> the friend
  Subject  : <sender> thinks DataCircles could help your team
  Body     :
    <sender> from <organisation> uses DataCircles to manage their sales
    and customer relationships, and thought it could be a good fit for
    your business.
    "<optional personal message>"
    In DataCircles you can manage customers and deals, sales pipelines,
    invoices and quotations, and reports and team collaboration.
    [See how it works]
    If you sign up and become a paying customer, both you and
    <organisation> receive a referral reward.
    This invitation was sent by <sender>. If it is not relevant to you,
    you can ignore this email.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

3.2  WALLET LOW BALANCE
  When     : Wallet balance drops to/below the reminder level set by the
             organisation (sent once per dip)
  From->To : DataCircles -> reminder email saved on the wallet
  Subject  : DataCircles wallet balance is running low
  Body (plain text):
    Your wallet balance has dropped to <balance> credits, at or below
    your reminder threshold of <threshold> credits. Add credits to avoid
    interruption.

4. TASKS & MEETINGS
(only to users who have that notification turned ON in settings)
################################################################

4.1  TASK ASSIGNED
  When     : Task created with assignees, or new assignee added
  From->To : DataCircles -> the assigned user
  Subject  : You've been assigned a task: "<task title>"
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    You have been assigned a task in DataCircles. The details are below.
      Task / Due date / Description / Related to
    Open the task in DataCircles to review it or update its status.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.2  TASK DUE REMINDER
  When     : Automatic, hourly - task due within next 24h and not completed
  From->To : DataCircles -> the task's user
  Subject  : Reminder: "<task title>" is due <date>
  Body     :
    This is a reminder that the following task is due on <date>.
      Task / Due date / Details
    Open it in DataCircles to update its status.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.3  MEETING SCHEDULED
  When     : A meeting is created
  From->To : DataCircles -> meeting creator, contact and participants
  Subject  : Meeting scheduled: <title>
  Body     :
    A meeting has been scheduled: <title>. The details are below.
      Date and time / Duration / Location / Type / Related record /
      Participants / Description
    Add it to your calendar so you have time to prepare.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.4  MEETING UPDATED
  When     : Meeting title/time/location/duration edited
  From->To : same people as 4.3
  Subject  : Meeting updated: <title>
  Body     :
    A meeting has been updated: <title>. The current details are below.
      Date and time / Duration / Location
    What changed:  <field>: <old> to <new>
    Update your calendar to match.
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.5  MEETING CANCELLED
  When     : Meeting cancelled
  From->To : same people as 4.3
  Subject  : Meeting cancelled: <title>
  Body     :
    The meeting <title> has been cancelled. You can remove it from your
    calendar.
      Was scheduled for / Duration / Location
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.6  MEETING COMPLETED
  When     : Meeting marked complete
  From->To : same people as 4.3
  Subject  : Meeting completed: <title>
  Body     :
    The meeting <title> is marked complete.
      Meeting / Was scheduled for / Notes / Outcome
    Regards, Team DataCircles

4.7  MEETING REMINDER
  When     : Meeting starts in ~1 hour. Sent when the "send reminders" API
             is called (no automatic cron calls it today)
  From->To : same people as 4.3
  Subject  : Reminder: <title> starts in 1 hour
  Body     :
    Your meeting <title> starts in about 1 hour.
      Date and time / Duration / Location / Type / Description
    Prepare any materials you need for this meeting.
    Regards, Team DataCircles


################################################################
5. DEALS
################################################################

5.1  DEAL STAGE CHANGED - STAFF
  When     : A deal moves to another stage
  From->To : DataCircles -> staff with deal notifications ON
  Subject  : Deal "<deal title>" moved to <new stage>
  Body     :
    Hello <name>,
    The deal <deal title> has moved from <old stage> to <new stage>.
      Deal title / Old status / New status / Amount / Company / Contact /
      Updated at
    Review the updated deal and take any next steps.
    Regards, Team DataCircles
  Note     : If the organisation created a custom template for this exact
             stage change, its own subject/body is used instead.
             Placeholders: {{userName}} {{dealTitle}} {{oldStatus}}
             {{newStatus}} {{amount}} {{companyName}} {{contactName}}
             {{updatedAt}}

5.2  DEAL STAGE CHANGED - CUSTOMER
  When     : Same event as 5.1, if the deal contact has an email
  From->To : Organisation (via DataCircles) -> deal contact
  Subject  : Update on <deal title> from <your company>
  Body     :
    Hello <contact name>,
    There is an update on <deal title>: it has moved from <old stage> to
    <new stage>.
      Deal / Previous status / Current status / Value
    We will be in touch with the next steps. Reply to this email if you
    have any questions.
    Regards, <your company>
  Note     : Custom template (see 5.1) overrides this if one exists.


################################################################
6. DOCUMENTS SENT TO CUSTOMERS
(sent by a DataCircles user to THEIR customer; replies go to the user;
 PDF attached; sign-off is the user's company name)
################################################################

6.1  QUOTATION
  When     : User clicks "Email quotation" (status becomes Sent)
  From->To : User's company -> deal/contact email
  Subject  : Quotation <number> from <company>
  Body     :
    Hello <contact>,
    Please find attached quotation <number>, dated <date>, for a total of
    <amount>.
    This quotation is valid until <date>.
    Let us know if you would like any changes.
      Quotation / Date / Total / Valid until
    Regards, <company>
  Attachment: Quotation-<number>.pdf

6.2  PROFORMA INVOICE
  When     : User emails a proforma invoice (status becomes Sent)
  From->To : User's company -> deal/contact email
  Subject  : Proforma Invoice <number> from <company>
  Body     :
    Hello <contact>,
    Please find attached proforma invoice <number>, dated <date>, for a
    total of <amount>.
    To confirm this order, please arrange payment as per the details on
    the invoice. Contact us if anything needs adjusting.
      Proforma invoice / Date / Total
    Regards, <company>
  Attachment: ProformaInvoice-<number>.pdf
  Note     : User can type their own subject/body instead.

6.3  DELIVERY CHALLAN
  When     : User emails a delivery challan (status becomes Sent)
  From->To : User's company -> deal contact email
  Subject  : Delivery Challan <number> from <company>
  Body     :
    Hello <contact>,
    Please find attached delivery challan <number>, dated <date>, covering
    the items dispatched to you.
      Delivery challan / Date
    Regards, <company>
  Attachment: DeliveryChallan-<number>.pdf

6.4  INVOICES (BULK)
  When     : User selects invoices > Send by email (one mail per customer)
  From->To : User's company -> customer email
  Subject  : Invoice(s) from <company>
  Body     :
    Hello <customer>,
    Please find attached <N> invoice(s) from <company>, totalling <total>.
    Payment terms and bank details are on each invoice. Reply to this
    email with any questions.
      Invoices / Total
    Regards, <company>
  Attachment: Invoice-<number>.pdf (one per invoice)

6.5  PAYMENT RECEIPT
  When     : User records a payment on an invoice with "notify by email" ticked
  From->To : User's company -> customer email
  Subject  : <company>: payment received for invoice <number>
  Body     :
    Hello <customer>,
    Thank you. We have recorded your payment against invoice <number>.
      Invoice number / Amount received / Payment date / Method /
      Reference-UTR / Balance on this invoice
    "This invoice is now fully paid."  OR
    "This is a part payment; <balance> remains outstanding."
    [Authorised signature image if set]
    This receipt is generated automatically. Please keep it for your records.
    Regards, <company>

6.6  ANY SALES / PURCHASE DOCUMENT (compose panel)
  When     : User emails a document (quotation, invoice, challan, sales
             return, purchase order, etc.), optional Cc/Bcc
  From->To : User's company -> typed recipient
  Subject  : <Document name> <number> from <company>   (editable)
  Body     :
    Hello <contact/vendor>,
    Please find attached <document name> <number>, dated <date>, for <amount>.
      <Document name> / Date / Amount
    Regards, <company>
  Note     : If the user types their own message, that text replaces the
             default body.

6.7  FREE-FORM EMAIL TO COMPANY / CONTACT / VENDOR
  When     : User composes an email from a company/contact/vendor record
  From->To : User -> that record's email
  Subject  : whatever the user types
  Body     : whatever the user types (no automatic greeting or sign-off)
